02 — About the artist

Looking closely.
Finding the character.

I am Hrithika D, an artist and painter working across portraiture, painting and mixed media.

My work moves between detailed charcoal drawings, expressive acrylic and watercolour paintings, and traditional subjects inspired by culture and everyday surroundings.

I am interested in expression, detail, colour and atmosphere—and in letting each medium bring its own character to the work.
